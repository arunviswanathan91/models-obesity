Beyond the assembled figures

This atlas provides custom views of the saved results rather than reproducing the paper’s figure panels. Search a feature, compare an available contrast, inspect diagnostics, or download the filtered table.

What the results can tell us

These are associations with BMI. Bulk RNA–protein concordance does not establish cell-specific protein expression. Conditional PTM effects are not direct measurements of occupancy. Simulations evaluate the RNA models, not PTM calibration.

Diagnostics and scope

Failed numerical checks are hidden from effect plots by default. Fine-immune categorical results are labelled provisional. Diagnostic summaries are available; draw-level chain traces and patient-level plots are not included in this release.

The original completed analyses are included. Unfinished remaining-analysis shards are excluded. No models are fitted by this website.
