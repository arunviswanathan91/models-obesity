How a signature catalogue is curated

A walkthrough of the rules used to review a catalogue of cell-type signatures: find redundant pairs, then screen proposed additions. Each change needs a person's decision.

Scripted replay. The catalogue is a small toy example and the replies are fixed text written in advance. Nothing here calls a model, uses a key or needs a network. It runs the same overlap rules as curate_signatures.py.
